# Texas College Academic Programs and Fees

## Undergraduate Programs

- Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT)
- Bachelor of Computer Science (BCS)
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
- Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM)

## Postgraduate Programs

- Master of Computer Science (MCS)
- MBA (Business Administration)
- MBA (Hospitality Management)

## Tuition and Fees

- Tuition fees at Texas College of Management & IT generally range between NPR 8.5 lakh and NPR 10.5 lakh for a 4-year bachelor's program, depending on the course.
- Exact postgraduate fee ranges are less consistently published, but align with private college averages in Kathmandu.
- Fees are payable in installments across semesters.

## Scholarships and Financial Aid

- Merit scholarships can cover up to 100% of tuition for top-performing students.
- Need-based support offers partial scholarships for students with financial constraints.
- Industry-linked sponsorships may provide stipends or fee support during internships.

## Key Notes

- Texas College is internationally affiliated with Lincoln University College, so credits are transferable abroad.
- Costs include access to labs, e-library, certifications, and career services.
- Certifications embedded in programs include CCNA, CEH, CHFI, AWS, Red Hat, ISO, Fortinet, and EC-Council Security Blue Team.
