# Texas College Events, Competitions, and Student Opportunities

## Major Events and Highlights

- TexHack 4.0: cybersecurity Capture the Flag competition.
- UI/UX Showdown and DevRumble: student technology competitions.
- Bootcamps: database design, UI/API integration, and Linux terminal skills.
- Freshers Fiesta 2026: student welcome event.
- Alumni Meet 2026: networking and community building.

## Student Development Activities

- Career Day connects students with recruiters and employers.
- Technical competitions promote practical innovation and teamwork.
- Bootcamps provide hands-on exposure to industry-relevant tools and workflows.

## Community and Alumni Network

Texas College emphasizes networking through alumni engagement and community events, helping students connect with graduates and industry stakeholders.
