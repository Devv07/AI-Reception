# Texas College of Management & IT (TCMIT) Overview

## Institutional Overview

- Founded by academics, corporate leaders, and entrepreneurs.
- Affiliated with Lincoln University College for internationally accredited courses and credit transfer.
- Located at Siphal, Chabahil, Kathmandu, Nepal.
- Sister unit of Texas International Education Network, established in 2010.

## Academic and Learning Environment

- Faculty includes professors, lecturers, and PhD scholars with national and international exposure.
- Multimedia classrooms support modern teaching.
- Specialized labs include AI, IoT, Robotics, VFX, CEH/CHFI, and SIEM/SOC.
- Learning resources include an e-library, Lincoln Learning Centre, journals, and reports.

## Career Development and Student Support

- Career Services Centre (CSC) provides job listings, counseling, interview preparation, and employer connections.
- Texas Jobs Portal is a dedicated platform for graduate employment.
- Career Day is an annual event connecting students with recruiters.
- Internships are mandatory across IT firms, banks, corporate organizations, and hotel chains.
- Soft skills training includes communication, leadership, CV writing, and interview skills.
